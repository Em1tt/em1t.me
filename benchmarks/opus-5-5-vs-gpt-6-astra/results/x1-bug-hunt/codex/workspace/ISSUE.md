# Bug reports from customers

We've collected these reports over the last month. Each is what a customer saw, in their words.

1. "We sell a cable for $19.99, and the invoice says $19.98. It's not just that item: something
   we price at $0.29 comes out as $0.28."
2. "Our SUMMER coupon is valid until June 30, but invoices issued on June 30 didn't get the
   discount."
3. "Customers who use a coupon are charged VAT on the full price instead of on the discounted
   price."
4. "The CSV export breaks when an item's name has a double quote in it, like `12" pipe`. The
   spreadsheet puts the row in the wrong columns."
5. "Credit notes show negative amounts as `$-5.00`. They should read `-$5.00`."
6. "Creating an invoice with no lines crashes. We create empty draft invoices and add the lines
   later."
7. "Some due dates are a day early. An invoice issued on 20 March 2026 with 14 days to pay says
   it's due on 2 April instead of 3 April."
